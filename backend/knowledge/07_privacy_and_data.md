# TechNova Privacy and Data

This document explains how TechNova handles customer data, exports and deletion.

## Data protection

### How data is protected
Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production data is limited to a small number of authorized engineers and is logged.

### Data sharing
TechNova does not sell customer data. Data is shared only with service providers that help us run the product, such as payment processors, and only as needed.

## Customer data rights

### Requesting a copy of your data
Customers can ask for a copy of all personal data we hold about them. A human agent verifies the identity of the requester, and the export is delivered within 30 days.

### Deleting an account and personal data
To delete an account and all personal data, the customer must contact support. A human agent verifies identity first. Deletion is completed within 30 days, and backups are fully purged within 90 days. Deletion cannot be undone.

### Data kept after cancelling
After a plan is cancelled, data is kept for 60 days so the customer can come back. After 60 days it is deleted permanently.

### Legal requests
Requests under privacy laws such as GDPR or India's data protection law are handled by our data protection team. The support assistant can explain the process but cannot complete these requests.
