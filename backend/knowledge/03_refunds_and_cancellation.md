# TechNova Refund and Cancellation Policy

This document explains when customers can get a refund and how to cancel a plan.

## Refund policy

### Full refund within 14 days
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first purchase of a paid plan. The request can be made by contacting support.

### Refunds after 14 days
After 14 days, monthly plans are not refunded for the current month. Annual plans can receive a refund for the full unused months, calculated from the date of the request.

### How long refunds take
Once a refund is approved, it reaches the original payment method in 5 to 7 business days. Refunds are always sent to the same payment method that was used for the purchase.

### Who approves refunds
Refund requests are reviewed and approved by the billing team. The support assistant can explain the policy but cannot approve or promise a refund.

### Refunds for add-ons
Extra storage add-ons are refunded only within 14 days of purchase and only if the extra storage is unused.

## Cancelling a plan

### How to cancel
Open Settings, choose Billing, and select Cancel plan. You can also ask support to cancel for you.

### What happens after cancelling
Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. After that, the account moves to a limited free state and your data is kept for 60 days.

### Cancelling to avoid the next charge
To avoid the next charge, cancel before the billing date. If you only want to stop automatic renewal, you can turn off auto renewal instead of cancelling.

## Alternatives to cancelling

### Pausing an annual plan
Annual customers can pause their subscription for up to 3 months. The pause extends the plan end date by the same period.

### Switching to a cheaper plan
If price is the concern, customers can downgrade, for example from Pro to Basic, at the start of the next billing period.

### Customers switching to a competitor
If a customer wants to cancel because of price or a competitor, the assistant can mention the pause option and the downgrade option. The final decision always stays with the customer.
