# TechNova Troubleshooting Guide

This document lists common technical problems and the first steps to fix them.

## Mobile app problems

### App crashes when opening a page
First update the app to the latest version. Then clear the app cache, restart the phone, and if needed uninstall and reinstall the app. If the crash continues, contact support with the error message and your phone model.

### Push notifications not arriving
Check that notifications are allowed for TechNova in your phone settings and that battery saving mode is not restricting the app. Then open Settings in the app, choose Notifications, and confirm they are turned on. Logging out and back in also refreshes the notification connection.

## Web app problems

### Supported browsers
The web app works on the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. Older browsers may show errors.

### Export to CSV fails or times out
Exports with more than 50,000 rows are processed in the background. You will receive an email with a download link within 15 minutes. For faster results, export a smaller date range.

### File upload fails
The maximum file size is 100 megabytes. If an upload fails near the end, check your internet connection and try again. Files in unsupported formats are rejected.

### Search returns no results
After a large import, the search index can take up to 15 minutes to update. If search still fails after that, sign out and sign in again and contact support if it continues.

### Dark mode not working
Dark mode follows your device settings by default. You can also choose a theme in Settings under Appearance.

## Outages

### Service down or error 503
If the whole workspace is down or shows a 503 error, check the TechNova status page first. A service outage is a critical issue. Business and Enterprise customers get 24 by 7 support for critical issues, and a human engineer is notified immediately.

### Integrations not syncing
If Slack or Google Drive stops syncing, disconnect the integration in Settings under Integrations and connect it again.
