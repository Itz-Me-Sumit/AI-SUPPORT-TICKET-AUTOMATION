# TechNova Account and Security

This document covers logging in, passwords, account changes and security.

## Passwords and login

### Resetting a password
On the login page choose Forgot password and follow the link sent to your email. The link works for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and try again after 10 minutes.

### Locked account
An account is locked after five wrong password attempts. It unlocks by itself after 30 minutes, or immediately after a password reset.

### Invalid credentials after a reset
If you still see an invalid credentials message after resetting your password, make sure you are using the email address the account was created with and that caps lock is off. If the problem continues, contact support so that an agent can verify your identity.

## Account changes

### Changing your email address
Open Settings, choose Profile, and select Email. A confirmation link is sent to the new address. All your data stays in the account when the email changes.

### Transferring account ownership
Only the account owner can transfer ownership. Open Settings, choose Team, and select Transfer ownership. The new owner must accept the request.

### Roles and permissions
There are three roles. Owners control billing and ownership. Admins manage users and settings. Members use the workspace without access to billing.

## Security

### Two factor authentication
To turn on two factor authentication, open Settings, choose Security, and select Two factor authentication. We support authenticator apps and text message codes.

### Single sign-on
Single sign-on is available on the Business and Enterprise plans.

### Suspected unauthorized access
If you see a login from a place you do not recognize, reset your password immediately, sign out of all devices from Settings under Security, and contact support. Suspected unauthorized access is treated as a critical issue and needs a human security specialist.

### What we never ask for
TechNova support will never ask for your password, one time codes or full card number on a call or in an email.
