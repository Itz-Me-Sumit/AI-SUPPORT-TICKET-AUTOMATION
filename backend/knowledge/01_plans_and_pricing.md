# TechNova Plans and Pricing

TechNova Workspace is a cloud platform where teams manage tasks, documents, chat and analytics in one place. This document explains our plans, prices and discounts.

## Basic plan

### What the Basic plan includes
The Basic plan costs 9 dollars per month and supports one user. It includes 5 gigabytes of storage, the web app and the mobile apps, and email support.

### Who the Basic plan is for
Basic is meant for individuals and freelancers. It does not include API access or priority support.

## Pro plan

### What the Pro plan includes
The Pro plan costs 29 dollars per month and supports up to 10 users. It includes 100 gigabytes of storage, API access, integrations with Slack and Google Drive, and priority support.

### Who the Pro plan is for
Pro is our most popular plan for small teams. A team of 5 people fits comfortably on Pro.

## Business plan

### What the Business plan includes
The Business plan costs 79 dollars per month and supports up to 50 users. It includes 1 terabyte of storage, single sign-on, advanced analytics, a dedicated account manager, and 24 by 7 support for critical issues.

### Group and team plans
TechNova does not have a plan called Group plan. For larger groups the right options are Pro for up to 10 users and Business for up to 50 users.

## Enterprise plan

### Enterprise pricing
Enterprise is for organizations with more than 50 users. Pricing is custom and is arranged by our sales team, so the support assistant cannot quote an Enterprise price.

## Annual billing and discounts

### Annual plan discount
Customers who pay yearly get two months free, which means they pay for 10 months. Annual prices are 90 dollars for Basic, 290 dollars for Pro and 790 dollars for Business.

### Student and education discount
Verified students and teachers get a 30 percent discount on Basic and Pro. The discount is applied after verification with a valid school email address.

### Free trial
Every new account gets a 14 day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is needed to start the trial.

## Changing plans

### Upgrading a plan
You can upgrade at any time from the Billing page in Settings. The new plan starts immediately and you pay only the prorated difference for the rest of the billing period.

### Downgrading a plan
A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period. Before downgrading, make sure your team size and storage fit within the lower plan limits.

### Extra storage
Additional storage can be added in blocks of 50 gigabytes for 5 dollars per month on any paid plan.
