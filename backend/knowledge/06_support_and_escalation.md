# TechNova Support and Escalation

This document explains how to reach support, how fast we respond, and when a human agent is needed.

## Support channels and hours

### Support hours
Live support is available Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 7 PM India Standard Time. Email support is available at all times.

### Response times by plan
For email support, Basic customers get a reply within 24 hours, Pro customers within 8 hours, and Business customers within 2 hours. Enterprise customers have a response time written in their contract.

### Critical issues outside business hours
Business and Enterprise customers can report critical issues at any time, 24 by 7. Other plans are handled in the next business day.

## Priority levels

### Critical priority
A ticket is critical when the service is down, many users are blocked, or a security incident is suspected. Critical tickets always go to a human agent and a first response is given within 1 hour.

### High priority
A ticket is high priority for money related problems such as duplicate charges, failed deliveries, or an account that is blocked. A human agent contacts the customer within 4 business hours.

### Medium and low priority
Medium priority covers important issues that do not block work. Low priority covers questions and requests for information. Many low priority questions can be answered directly by the assistant.

## Assistant and human agents

### What the AI assistant can do
The AI assistant answers questions about plans, pricing, policies, account steps and troubleshooting, and it creates a support ticket for every call.

### What needs a human agent
Refund approvals, billing disputes, security incidents, account deletion, and custom Enterprise pricing always need a human agent. The assistant never approves refunds or gives discounts.

### When the assistant does not know
If the answer is not in the knowledge base, the assistant says so politely and does not guess. The question is recorded in the ticket so a human can follow up.

### Escalation
When a ticket is escalated, a specialist reviews the original request, the call transcript and the suggested action before contacting the customer.
