# TechNova Billing and Payments

This document explains how billing works, which payment methods we accept, and what to do when something looks wrong on a bill.

## Payment methods

### Accepted payment methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI and PayPal. UPI is available for customers in India. We do not accept cash, cheques or bank transfers for self-service plans.

### Updating a payment method
Open Settings, choose Billing, and select Payment method. The new method is used from the next billing date.

## Billing cycle

### When you are charged
You are charged on the same date every month, or once a year for annual plans. The billing date is the day you first subscribed.

### Invoices
An invoice is emailed after every successful payment. You can also download all invoices from Settings under Billing and then Invoice history.

### Taxes
Taxes such as GST or VAT are added to the price at checkout based on your billing country. The tax amount is shown separately on every invoice.

## Billing problems

### Charged twice or duplicate charge
If you see the same charge twice, contact support. Our billing team verifies the duplicate and refunds the extra charge in full. Once approved, the refund reaches the original payment method in 5 to 7 business days. A duplicate charge is treated as a high priority billing issue and needs a human billing specialist.

### Charge you do not recognize
If you see a charge you did not authorize, do not wait. Contact support right away and a billing specialist will investigate. Unauthorized charges are treated as high priority.

### Wrong amount on an invoice
If an invoice shows the wrong billing address, company name, tax number or tax amount, our billing team can issue a corrected invoice. Corrections are possible within 30 days of the invoice date.

### Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry it automatically three times over seven days and send an email each time. If all retries fail, the account becomes read only until the payment method is fixed. No data is deleted.

### Price changes
We give at least 30 days notice by email before any price change. Your current price stays the same until the notice period ends.

### Auto renewal
Plans renew automatically. You can turn off auto renewal in Settings under Billing. The plan then stays active until the end of the paid period.
